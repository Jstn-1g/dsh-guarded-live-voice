# Code of conduct

DSH Live Voice welcomes contributors and testers with different backgrounds,
experience levels, accessibility needs, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Make room for questions and explain project-specific assumptions.
- Protect credentials, recordings, transcripts, workspace content, and personal
  information.
- Distinguish reproduced facts from hypotheses, and preview behavior from
  released guarantees.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of
private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may restrict
participation when necessary to protect people or the project. For a private
conduct report, contact `info@eversko.com`. Security vulnerabilities should use
the repository's private security-advisory flow instead.
